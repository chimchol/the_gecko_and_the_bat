# 🦎 The Gecko and the Bat

**[▶ Play the game](https://chimchol.github.io/the_gecko_and_the_bat/)**

Help the gecko catch flies in the desert, but watch out for the bat!

## How to play

- **Arrow keys** to move the gecko
- The gecko's tongue catches flies in front of it
- Every fly makes the gecko a little faster
- Every 10 flies, a bat appears. A red square shows where it will come out, so get away! Survive 10 seconds and it flies off
- Don't run into the walls

**P** pauses the game, **M** turns sound on or off, **Space** starts a new game after game over.
